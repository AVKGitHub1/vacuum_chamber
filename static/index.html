PLACEMENT GUIDE

One focus. Two angles.

Focal point elevation
Height from the bottom flange face to the focus on the chamber axis.
Focal length
Distance along the port axis from the focus to the outer flange sealing face.
Alpha · 0–360°
Rotation around the chamber, counterclockwise from +X in top view.
Beta · 45–135°
Angle from the vertical chamber axis. 90° is a horizontal radial port; below 90° points upward.

The model uses Z as its vertical axis. All saved dimensions use millimeters; switching display units does not change geometry.
